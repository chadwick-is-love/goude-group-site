§1

Accounting is not optimized by tools. It is optimized by proof.

Every firm is being sold software. None of it answers the only question a partner has: is the output right, and who says so.

The market sells effort. Workshops that end in applause, prompt libraries, project fees with no acceptance test, and nobody’s name on whether the numbers are right.

A tie-out is the answer this profession already trusts: a claim about a period, made against figures the firm produced before anyone arrived. It is the form we chose to be measured in, and a firm may measure us with it.

§2

The Verified Close Standard.

VCS-1, edition 2026.1, effective 2026-09-01. Open, free to reproduce with attribution, written to be run against any vendor. This one included.

§2.1

The Known-Good Month

The firm picks a closed period it trusts. The system runs it cold. The output ties to the penny or the build does not go live. No partial pass.

BasisClient-selected
§2.2

Payment follows the proof

Half at complete intake, half when the period ties. The balance is never paid against a promise.

Split50 / 50
§2.3

The Tie-Out

One signed document per period: reconciled, every exception listed and resolved. Firms send it to clients. Clients send it to auditors.

BasisSigned artifact
§2.4

The period is kept

When a major model version ships, every covered build is re-run against its known-good period. A model change becomes a letter to the client, not a discovery inside a close.

BasisRetained, re-run
§2.5

Certification

Firms that pass carry the mark, annually, naming the systems covered. Vendors whose exports hold their shape carry verified status. The mark asserts one thing: tested against a period somebody had already closed.

Term12 months
§2.6

Stated limits

No claim of silent monitoring, because that promise cannot be kept. The client flags a change or the period review catches it, and the artifact records which.

ScopeStated

§5

The Tie-Out, weekly.

One letter a week for people who have to be right. An instrument you can run that week, one thing that broke in a real firm and what it cost, one figure that had to reconcile, and one line of what is open. Never two. Issue 01 is dated 2026-09-15.

No roundups, no model news. Every issue keeps a permanent address.
