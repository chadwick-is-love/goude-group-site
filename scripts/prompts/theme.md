# THE THEME, FOR ONE ISSUE

**APPLIES TO: issue No. 007 only.**

If the issue you are building is not No. 007, this file is spent. Ignore every word of it and run `scripts/prompts/research.md` unmodified. A theme has never become a standing beat on this publication and this one does not either. Nos. 003 and 004 narrowed to a single field with no ruling behind them, and one issue's approval got mistaken for a rule; that is the failure this scope line exists to prevent.

## OPERATORS WEEK

The default rule ranks the week's developments by consequence to a small operator. Operators Week keeps that rule and tightens the test one turn: a story earns a slot only if a business under fifty people, with no general counsel, no CFO and no procurement department, can act on it before the next issue.

Same cadence. Same eleven domains, all of them in scope, none of them the subject. What changes is the threshold and the shape of the five.

### The threshold

Rank by what the reader can do, not by the size of the number. A development worth $400,000 to a 500-person firm and nothing to a twelve-person one does not run this week, however large it sounds. A filing deadline that costs a nine-person shop $2,100 if they miss it does.

Every one of the five must survive this test: name the person inside a small business who does the thing, name what they touch, name the week they have to do it in. If that person is a department the reader does not employ, the story is out.

### The shape of the five

Cover the operating surface, not the news cycle. One slot each, filled from whichever domain produced it this week:

1. **What the operator pays.** A cost, a rate, a premium, a price that moved.
2. **What the operator owes.** A filing, a rule, a deadline, an exposure with a date on it.
3. **Who the operator staffs.** Labor, wages, hiring, classification, benefits.
4. **How the operator sells.** Demand, channel, platform terms, payments and fraud.
5. **What the operator runs on.** Software pricing, vendor consolidation, tooling, technology.

The mapping to the eleven domains is free. Insurance can fill slot 1 or slot 2 depending on what actually happened. Technology fills slot 5 only if a real price, term or date moved; general commentary about AI fills nothing, this week or any week.

If the week genuinely produced nothing in one of the five, take the strongest remaining story that clears the threshold and say in `why_it_ranks` which slot went empty and why.

### Out this week

Enterprise-only developments. Funding rounds. Vendor announcements with no SMB-tier price and no date. Macro punditry. Anything whose action item requires a department the reader does not have. Anything already covered in Nos. 001 through 006 without a material, dated change.

### The instruments

Each article already carries a calculator or a generator. This week every one of them prices or produces something at the reader's own scale: their headcount, their premium, their balance, their invoice. No instrument may ask for a number a small operator would not have on hand in under a minute.

### The register

Second person throughout, as always. The standfirst says what moved across the operating surface, in article order.

Do not add a theme banner and do not print the words "Operators Week" anywhere on the page. Page furniture is fixed by `scripts/lib/templates.mjs`, the Social Engine parses that markup, and a label there is a build change, not an editorial one. The theme is a selection instrument, not a banner.
