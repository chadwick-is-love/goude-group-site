How we calculate return

Every system shows its math. Defaults are set for a business doing roughly $1M to $5M a year. Replace them with your own numbers; the page recalculates.

The two formulas we use

Revenue systems: jobs recovered × average ticket × your gross margin = monthly return

Time systems: hours saved × hourly cost × 70% = monthly return

We count revenue at your margin, because a recovered $350 job is not $350 of profit. We count 70 percent of time saved, because a freed hour is not a paid-out hour. Year one is twelve months of return minus the build price and twelve months of running cost. Every figure is a range: the low end assumes 60 percent of the expected result.

Your numbers, not ours

The sliders, margin included, start conservative. If your missed calls, ticket or margin are higher, the return is higher, and the reverse.

Questions buyers ask

Plain answers on ownership, tools, running costs and what happens after launch.

Some of these are already in my software. Why pay for them?

Missed-call text back, review requests and appointment reminders come with GoHighLevel, Jobber, Housecall Pro and ServiceTitan. Receipt capture and invoice reminders come with QuickBooks and Xero. Most businesses own them and never turn them on. Those five are priced as switch-ons: we switch on what you already own, write the wording, file the texting registration with you, and tune it for 30 days. Where you have nothing, we supply it.

How long does texting take to go live?

Carriers approve every business that texts, under its own business: legal name and tax ID, a website with privacy and terms, a consent form and sample messages. We file it with you. Approval takes from a few days to a few weeks. Only Missed-Call Text Back waits on it, and its build clock starts when it clears. Reviews, reminders, speed-to-lead, reactivation and referrals go live by email on schedule and add texting once it is approved.

Do I need new software?

No. The systems run on our own automation and a phone line we hold, connected to what you already use: your phone number, calendar, CRM, QuickBooks or Xero, Google profile and website. If you already use GoHighLevel, we connect to it like any other tool. We do not move you onto new software. Where a piece is missing, we supply it inside the build.

What does it cost to run each month?

Running costs are passed through at cost: text messages, email sending, voice minutes. For most businesses this is $20 to $150 a month across a bundle. Each system lists its own, and every return figure on this page already deducts it.

Who owns the data and the system?

Your customer data stays yours, in your accounts. The build runs for your business and keeps running after the tuning period.

What happens after the 30 days of tuning?

The system keeps running. Care is priced by the size of each build, at most $149 a month for builds up to $500, $249 up to $1,000, $349 up to $2,000, and $499 above $2,000, which covers every bundle. A single system's care is never more than half of what it returns on conservative numbers, so some cost less, and care across several single systems stops at $499 a month. Care Partner is $999 with $500 a month of credit toward new systems. It covers wording and timing changes, script refreshes, a monthly numbers report, fixes within two business days, and we carry the texting and voice accounts so you never reconcile those invoices. New systems are a new build.

Will customers know they are talking to an automated system?

Yes when it matters. The phone agent and chat identify themselves as an assistant for your business. Texts and emails go out in your wording, from your business.

We are a franchise. Can this run across locations?

Yes. Each location gets its own numbers, routing and reporting, and the Owner Dashboard shows them side by side. Where each location has its own owner, each one files its own texting registration. Franchise approval requirements are handled at kickoff.

Can I pay monthly instead of up front?

Yes, on every bundle. Essential is $749 a month, Growth $1,099 and Operator $1,299, for 12 months with no setup and care included. After 12 months it rolls down to bundle care at $499. Paying monthly costs about 6 to 11 percent more in year one.

Can I start with one system and add more?

Yes. If you later complete a bundle, the bundle price applies and what you already paid counts toward it.
