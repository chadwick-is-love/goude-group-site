One build, or a partner that keeps building.

The Operational Review

The way in for any serious buyer.

An owner who knows something is broken but cannot name the build starts here. It costs them about five or six hours, ends with a priced plan, and most clients move into a retainer from it.

Week 1Assess

Each team lead takes a 20-minute assessment covering strategy, data, operations, billing and finance. We map the software they already pay for.

Weeks 1 to 2Interview

One-hour interviews with each lead, the CEO and the founder. We find where hours and margin leak and why.

Weeks 2 to 3Readout

A live presentation of the findings. The return on each recommendation is calculated in front of them with their own hourly costs.
