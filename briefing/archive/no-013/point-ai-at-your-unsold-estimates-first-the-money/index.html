Point AI at your unsold estimates first. The money is there.

What moved

On October 5, Dallas startup UpSmith raised a $10 million Series A. Its AI agents chase open estimates, missed calls and lapsed memberships for home-services firms. It says it serves more than 200 operators. Its own numbers: Medley AC in Texas booked $147,000 in new revenue in three months and cut cancellations 25%. In late September, ServiceTitan added a Virtual Agent that texts customers about unsold estimates.

What it changes

Every business that quotes has a pile of estimates nobody chased. The customer asked for a price, went quiet, and your office moved on. That pile is the cheapest revenue you own, because the demand already exists. AI does the dull part: the second text, the third, the answer to the warranty question at 9pm. You are not buying leads. You are collecting on work you already priced.

What not to do

Do not treat UpSmith's $147,000 as your number; it is the vendor's figure for a customer it named in a funding announcement. Do not run two follow-up sequences at once; ServiceTitan warns its new agent and its older Unsold Estimates campaign will double-message the same customer. Do not let AI discount or change scope; price stays with a person. Do not text anyone who never gave you a number for it.

The pile you already paid for

An unsold estimate is the cheapest revenue in the building. You already paid for the site visit, the drive and the takeoff. The customer already told you they want the work. What kills the deal is silence on day three and day ten, because your office is busy with today's calls. That is a memory problem, and software never forgets a date. It also never feels awkward sending the third message, which is the one a busy office always skips.

Start with what you already run. ServiceTitan now has the follow-up built in for Core, Pro and Max accounts, in private preview. On Jobber, Housecall Pro or a spreadsheet, a Zapier or Make flow plus ChatGPT or Claude drafts each message from the estimate notes. A person approves the first fifty before anything sends on its own. A dedicated tool like UpSmith works your whole customer history and answers calls, but you pay for another platform and hand it your customer data. Run the cheap version for thirty days, count booked jobs, then decide.

The play

  1. Your office manager, Monday: export every estimate from the last 90 days that did not close. Name, phone, job, amount, date sent.
  2. Sort by dollar value. A person calls the top twenty this week. Everything else goes into the AI sequence.
  3. Paste into ChatGPT or Claude with the list: "Here are my unsold estimates with job notes. Write a day 2, day 7 and day 21 text for each. Under 300 characters, plain words, one question each, no discounts."
  4. On ServiceTitan, ask your rep for the Virtual Agent unsold estimates goal, and switch off the older Unsold Estimates campaign for those business units first.
  5. Everyone else builds it in Zapier or Make: estimate marked unsold, wait two days, ChatGPT drafts the text, a person taps approve, it sends.
  6. Use The Quote Chaser on this page to write the sequence and house rules. Count booked jobs after 30 days.
